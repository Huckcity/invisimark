What happens to your files

{{retention_paragraph}}

Files you send for checking

A file sent to the AI marking checker is used only to look for marks. It is deleted when the check finishes or fails, and is never kept, even when the paragraph above says other uploads are. The result holds the file's SHA-256 hash and what was found, not the file or its name. It is kept for {{ttl}} so the page can show it, and then removed.

Keys and names

The secret key for a job is created for that job. It is in the results for the same limited time and in the case file you download. It is not stored after that, so the service cannot recover it. The names you type are put in the case file and are removed with the rest of the job.

Accounts, cookies, logs

There are no accounts. This site sets no cookies of its own. Your address is held in memory while a job runs, to limit each person to one job at a time, and is not written to disk by the application. The service is configured not to keep access logs; the hosting provider's network may log connections under its own policy.

Advertising

This site may show advertisements served by third parties. They can set cookies and see your address and the page you are on, under their own policies. They never see your files, which are not part of the pages they appear on.
